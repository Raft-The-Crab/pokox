# Community Guidelines (draft)

## 1. Scope

These guidelines apply to chat, user names, game content, marketplace listings and all other user-authored material in PokoX, Poko Studio and Poko Mod.

## 2. Expected conduct

- Treat other players and contributors with respect.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or impersonate others.
- Do not share personal information belonging to someone else.
- Do not submit sexually explicit content involving minors. Such content is removed and reported as required by law.

## 3. Prohibited conduct

- Cheating that alters validated state, or distributing tools to do so.
- Farming grants or payouts with bots, alt accounts or device clusters.
- Wash trading and other market manipulation.
- Ban evasion through device graphs or shared accounts.
- Harassing moderators or attempting to determine their identity.

## 4. Moderation process

Detectors and reports create cases. A moderator claims a case with a single-use device-bound key. Punitive actions need two distinct moderator votes. A claim and an approval may not come from the same key. Players receive a reason code and a case reference, and may appeal within 30 days.

## 5. Enforcement ladder

Warning, chat mute, temporary ban (1/7/30 days), permanent ban, asset removal, game takedown, share-trade suspension, payout hold. No single signal produces an automatic ban.

## 6. Taxonomy status

The full guideline taxonomy with examples is still a known shallow area in the plan. Counsel and the community council advise on it; the council has no authority over bans, the ledger or releases.
