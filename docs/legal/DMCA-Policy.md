# DMCA Policy (draft)

## 1. Registered agent

A DMCA agent must be registered before launch (LEG-04). Contact details will be published in `security.txt` and the footer of every app.

## 2. Notice

A notice must identify the copyrighted work, the material and its location (game id, word, asset CID), the complainant's contact details, a statement of good-faith belief, and a statement under penalty of perjury that the notice is accurate and the complainant is authorised to act.

## 3. Action

A valid notice triggers a takedown: the live pointer moves in one transaction, the release is marked taken down, an audit event is written, caches are invalidated, and share holders are refunded per the Marketplace and Exchange Terms. Ledger conservation is preserved.

## 4. Counter-notice

A counter-notice must include the same identifying information, a statement under penalty of perjury, and consent to jurisdiction. Material is restored after the statutory waiting period unless a court order is provided.

## 5. Repeat infringers

Repeat infringer accounts are terminated under the Terms of Service. Policy versioning uses a public changelog.
