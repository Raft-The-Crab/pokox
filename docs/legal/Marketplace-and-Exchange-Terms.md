# Marketplace and Exchange Terms (draft)

## 1. Terminology

Use "support a game", never "invest". Shares are virtual support units inside PokoX. They are not securities, not real-money instruments, and carry no promise of return. Dividends are Pokoins redeemable only inside PokoX.

## 2. Eligibility

A game may list after 30 days, 500 active users, 1,000 CCU-hours and a clean record. Access is 18+ pending counsel review. No leverage, shorting, real-money buy-in or cash-out.

## 3. Supply and pricing

10,000 shares per game; developer retains 20% locked for 180 days. Bonding curve `P(s) = 2 * (1 + s/4000)^2` Pokoins. v1 is bonding curve only; an order book v2 requires manipulation data.

## 4. Tax, dividends and caps

15% seller-side tax flows to that game's pool. Weekly dividends come from that pool, pro rata, using exact floor math. Position caps: 5% per game, 20 games, 10,000 Pokoins per game. Lock-ups: 7 days user, 180 days developer. Circuit breaker: ±10% per 24 hours on secondary trades.

## 5. Perks

10 badge, 100 cosmetic, 500 title, 1,000 shareholder server, 2,500 quarterly vote. Perks are cosmetic or advisory; they never alter the ledger, bans or releases.

## 6. Misconduct

Wash trades, self-trading and manipulation create cases in Poko Mod and may be reversed as new balanced ledger transactions. Delisting after 90 days offline refunds the last price minus 15%.

## 7. Conservation

Pokoins and share ledgers move atomically. No orphan transactions are permitted. Client-reported share counts are never trusted.
