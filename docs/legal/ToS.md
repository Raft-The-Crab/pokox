# Terms of Service (draft)

## 1. Parties and eligibility

PokoX is operated by a registered entity (name and jurisdiction to be confirmed by counsel). An account is required for online play. Guest access permits cached single-player only. Users under 13 require parental consent; users 13–17 have restricted chat and no Exchange access.

## 2. Nature of the service

PokoX provides software for creating and playing user-made games. The platform is offered at no monetary cost. There is no purchase of Pokoins, no cash-out, and no real-money consideration between users.

## 3. User obligations

- Do not interfere with the service, other users, or the authority validation system.
- Do not submit unlawful, infringing, or abusive content.
- Do not attempt to bypass publishing gates, moderation, or account restrictions.
- Do not automate accounts to obtain grants or payouts.

## 4. Virtual currency

Pokoins are virtual units with no cash value, non-transferable outside PokoX, and authoritative ledger entries control balances. Lost or erased balances are not restored outside the published reversal process. See `Virtual-Currency-and-Refund-Terms.md`.

## 5. User content and licences

Users retain copyright in their games and assets and grant PokoX a licence to host, transmit and display them for the purpose of operating the platform. Removal follows the published DMCA and takedown process.

## 6. Enforcement

Suspicion signals create a review case; there is no instant automated ban. Punitive actions require two distinct moderator votes. Reasons are given as codes, without disclosing moderator identity. Appeals are available for 30 days.

## 7. Changes

Material changes require notice. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance, subject to counsel review of that clause.

## Open questions for counsel

- Operating entity, jurisdiction and governing law.
- Whether the two-vote requirement creates any procedural obligation.
- Enforceability of reason-code-only notices.
