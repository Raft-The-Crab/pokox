# Children's Privacy Notice (draft)

## 1. Age gates

Users under 13 require verifiable parental consent and have no chat or Exchange access, with capped grants. Users 13–17 have no Exchange access, filtered chat and a lower spend cap. All gates are enforced server-side, never by the client.

## 2. Information collected

For a child account we collect only what is needed to operate the service and enforce the age tier: an account identifier, parental consent record where required, and minimal necessary telemetry. Telemetry is disabled for minors and guests.

## 3. No profiling

There is no behavioural advertising, no ad profiling, and no sale of data. Positional history and chat content are never included in telemetry.

## 4. Retention and deletion

Identity data is retained for the account life plus 30 days. Ledger entries are permanent and pseudonymised on deletion to preserve conservation. A parent may request access, correction or deletion of their child's data.

## 5. Contact

A dedicated privacy contact is required before launch. Complaints may also be raised with the relevant supervisory authority.
